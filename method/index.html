02 / Process

Curiosity first.
Evidence always.

LLMs help me navigate large systems and generate adversarial hypotheses. They never get the final vote—runtime proof does.

  1. IObserve

    Follow the boundaries.

    Trace how applications handle trust, and where an assumption starts to break.

  2. IIVerify

    Make the proof clear.

    Build focused tools and reproducible tests that show what an attacker can actually do.

  3. IIIDisclose

    Help the fix happen.

    Share precise evidence, explain the impact, and work through responsible disclosure.

01

Understand the promise

I start with people, assets, authority, and the boundary that is supposed to hold. A threat model keeps the hunt pointed at consequences that matter.

what must stay true?
02

Trace one thin path

I follow one attacker-controlled source through its guards and transformations to one sensitive sink. Focus makes assumptions visible.

source → sink
03

Build the cheapest oracle

Exact artifacts, positive controls, direct-boundary probes, and guarded negatives separate product behavior from a persuasive-looking coincidence.

try to kill the idea
04

Prove the human impact

A primitive becomes a finding only when the attacker gains something and the victim demonstrably loses something. Limitations belong in the report.

proof, not theatre
05

Disclose responsibly

I package a reproducible path, evidence, root cause, and a practical fix—then give the vendor room to investigate before anything becomes public.

make fixing easier

The model can suggest where to look.
The evidence decides what is real.

Retak means a crack. That is where we start.

RETAK Security is an independent research studio from Indonesia, focused on browser trust boundaries, Android and mobile application security, and practical web exploitation.

Research with proof attached.

RETAK works across bug bounty, source review, browser security, and mobile application research. Its public record includes CVEs affecting Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and related Apple platforms.

The studio combines manual security judgment with LLM-assisted threat modeling and source-to-sink analysis. Every candidate stays behind reproduction, guarded-negative, and impact gates until the evidence earns a stronger claim.

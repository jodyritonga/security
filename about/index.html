I’m Jody Ritonga—a security researcher and builder in Tangerang. I like messy systems, precise evidence, and finding the small assumption that changes everything.

I hunt conventional vulnerabilities with a modern toolkit.

My work spans browser security, Android and mobile applications, and web application testing. I use LLMs as research partners for threat modeling and source analysis—not as a substitute for technical judgment.

I’m happiest when an ambiguous behavior becomes a clean source-to-sink story, a reproducible PoC, and a report that helps someone ship a real fix.

Catch that response ! (A tale of leaked URL reset password in Response)

Hello everyone ! It’s good to update my medium blog, can’t wait to share with you guys another Bug that i’ve found ! So for all newcomers i hope you enjoy my writing. I hope this writeup can increase your skill and confidence in bug hunting. Before we get into Catch that response ! (A tale of leaked URL reset password in Response). I just want to make clear that Im also new and still striving to learn about technology and bug hunting world. So if you find any misinformation, please dont hesitate to fix it in the comment. It’s make me happy when someone correcting my mistake :D

Brief explaination of the bug

So what kind of bug do i found? Well my title is already self explain it, so this bug occur when the developer forgot to wrap up the response from the server. Sometimes when developer trying to hide secret response from backend. they forgot to also protect the frontend to see it. For example like OTP.

Maybe the developer already provide security mechanism to hide it in the backend, but they forgot to also protect it on the frontend, so malicious actor can see the OTP in the response without need to actually have the number / account that should receive the OTP. Because this bug is pretty simple, and didn’t need much explaination, so just lets dive into the writeup and the weirdness of the server.

Write up

Im not a fan of automation scanning like nuclei etc. So my methodology when i approaching target is try to poke the feature that make you curious. For me i always curious about the login / register page feature. I love to exploring on that area. Usually i’ve tried low hanging bugs like no rate limit, password reset poisoning, etc.

But this time. I’ve feel very shock when i test this website let me tell you why

  1. So i’ve already set my burp up, and try to poke around the feature from the forgot password section.
  2. I fill my email, and before i click ok. i turn on my burpsuite intruder, catch the response and send it to the repeater.

3. After sending it to the repeater, I clicked ‘send.’ I couldn’t believe my eyes when I saw the response — I was like, ‘No way this is happening… no way! Did God just give me free gold or something?’ It was just so unreal and relaxing at the same time!

4. I know guys ! all of your hacker sense is super tingly right? This is a critical vulnerability ! I just need to know your email, and boom the next day your account it’s not your’s anymore. After that i make the report into the company and only need 1 days for them to fix it and of course they appreciate me enough :D

Conclusion

Before u startup all of ur automation stuff. Always remember to poke it first. Manual testing is much fun, and sometimes it’s surprise you for the randomness or the weirdness of the response the u’ve found on the website.

Always check the response. Look closely, learn how the website response for your request. You need this kind of mindset “ If i supply the website A what is the response of the website? Did the website response correctly?” So always curious. Remember don’t let duplicate stop you and keep hunting hackers ! Dont worry about the money. When your skill increasing the money will flow by itself.

See you on the next post hunters !
